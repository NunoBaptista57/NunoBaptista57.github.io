Project Overview

HPV Combate Viral (Project L)

Visual Development, UI/UX Design & 2D Frame-by-Frame Animation

Role: Concept Artist, UI/UX Designer, 2D Animator

Timeline: June 2023 – February 2024

Tools: Photoshop, Illustrator, Unity

About the Project

HPV Combate Viral (internally developed as Project L) is an educational serious game designed to spread awareness about the Human Papillomavirus (HPV), its transmission pathways, prevention methods, and age group implications through accessible card-battler gameplay mechanics.

My work encompassed visual development across character design, frame-by-frame 2D animation, interface and card illustration, halftone environments, visual effects, and promotional design.

01. Protagonist Character Design

Character development began with a discussion about how to make the high-school protagonist relatable to players of all genders. Early exploration focused on casual clothing and contemporary hairstyles.

Following team feedback, the team chose an androgynous direction and a distinctive mullet, reflecting that HPV affects people of all sexes while avoiding a character-selection system that was outside the game's focus.

02. 2D Frame-by-Frame Character Animation

Once the protagonist's design was established, I developed 2D sprite loops to bring combat interactions to life:

  • Idle Loop: Subtle weight shifts and leg adjustments for a natural standing loop.
  • Card Attack Motion: A dynamic card throw inspired by comic-book action.
  • Damage Feedback: A clear reaction pose when the character takes damage.

Additional Animation Studies

03. Vector Card Illustrations

To communicate medical concepts clearly, hand-drawn sketches were converted into vector artwork with a consistent visual language across card categories:

  • Transmission Cards: Playful representations of sexual, skin-to-skin, and vertical transmission.
  • Prevention Cards: Educational scenes, including the protagonist explaining HPV and a virus-containment concept.
  • Age Incidence Cards: Metaphorical concepts for target age demographics.

04. UI/UX Design & System Architecture

I structured visual assets for Unity integration, covering menus, icons, button guides, typography, and combat feedback:

  • Typography: Explored Walter Turncoat for hand-drawn text and Londrina styles for headings.
  • Interface Assets: Designed health bars, 9-slice button textures, status icons, and player name-entry elements.
  • Tutorial and Feedback: Developed tutorial panels and arrow-color options, plus readable popup states for protection, Ultimate activation, and correct or incorrect answers.

05. Combat Environments & VFX Integration

For the primary combat arena, I developed a halftone texture treatment to establish depth while keeping the characters and interface readable. The background artwork was organized into layers for parallax and effects.

I also explored an energy-aura effect for the Ultimate ability, designed to overlay the protagonist across animation states. Later project reviews included further Ultimate-effect and tutorial work.

06. Promotional Design

In November 2023, I iterated on a project poster and store banner. The review focused on logo placement and whether to bring the slime and card motifs from the banner into the poster, helping the promotional pieces feel consistent with the game's visual identity.
