Project Overview

HPV Combate Viral (Project L)

Visual Development, UI/UX Design & 2D Frame-by-Frame Animation

Role: Concept Artist, UI/UX Designer, 2D Animator

Timeline: June 2023 – February 2024

Tools: Photoshop, Illustrator, Unity

About the Project

HPV Combate Viral (internally developed as Project L) is an educational serious game designed to spread awareness about the Human Papillomavirus (HPV), its transmission pathways, prevention methods, and age group implications through accessible card-battler gameplay mechanics[cite: 1].

My work encompassed full visual development—from initial protagonist ideation and frame-by-frame 2D action animation loops to UI systems design, vector card illustration, halftone environment art, VFX creation, and promotional marketing graphics[cite: 1].

01. Protagonist Character Design

Character development began in late June 2023 with initial discussions regarding the visual identity of our high school-aged protagonist[cite: 1]. Early iterations explored standard casual streetwear (hoodie, sweatshirt, long pants) and trendy hairstyles[cite: 1].

Following team feedback, the design pivoted toward an androgynous protagonist featuring a distinct mullet haircut to maximize player relatability and representation[cite: 1].

Preliminary concept art sheets exploring body types, face shapes, outfits, and color palettes[cite: 1].

02. 2D Frame-by-Frame Character Animation

Once the protagonist's design was locked, I developed full 2D sprite loops to bring combat interactions to life[cite: 1]:

  • Idle Loop: Subtle weight shifts and leg adjustments for a natural standing loop (AnimTest8.gif)[cite: 1].
  • Card Attack Motion: A dynamic slashing card throw inspired by Gambit (attack_1.gif / attack_2.gif)[cite: 1].
  • Damage Feedback: High-impact reaction pose when taking damage (hit_1.gif)[cite: 1].

03. Vector Card Illustrations

To communicate medical concepts clearly, hand-drawn sketches were converted into clean, vibrant vector artwork maintaining visual consistency across card categories[cite: 1]:

  • Transmission Cards: Playful representations including viruses holding hands (Sexual), high-fiving (Skin-to-Skin), and a parent virus holding a baby virus (Vertical Transmission)[cite: 1].
  • Prevention Cards: Educational scenes including the protagonist pointing at a virus chalkboard drawing ("Educate about HPV") and a virus "containment breach" concept[cite: 1].
  • Age Incidence Cards: Metaphorical concepts for target age demographics ("15 to 24 years old")[cite: 1].

04. UI/UX Design & System Architecture

I structured the visual asset list for Unity integration, covering menus, icons, button guides, and typography[cite: 1]:

  • Typography: Implemented Google Fonts Walter Turncoat for general hand-drawn text and Londrina Shadow for headers[cite: 1].
  • Interface Assets: Designed custom HP bars, 9-slice button base textures, status icons (health, protection, score, settings), and player name entry boxes[cite: 1].
  • Combat HUD & Dialogues: Created pop-up modals ("Not Enough Protection", "Ultimate Active") and integrated comic-style floating impact feedback ("POW")[cite: 1].

Combat interface elements and card presentation[cite: 1].

05. Combat Environments & VFX Integration

For the primary combat arena background, I developed a halftone/patterned texture style to establish depth while maintaining high contrast for character silhouettes and UI readability[cite: 1]. Artwork was exported in modular layers (foreground, midground, background) for parallax and particle effects[cite: 1].

Additionally, I created dynamic "Ultimate" ability visual effects—designing an energy aura wave that seamlessly overlays the protagonist across all animation states (idle, attack, and hit reactions)[cite: 1].

Modular arena environment prepared for Unity rendering and depth layering[cite: 1].

06. Marketing & Promotional Materials

During the final polish phase, I designed promotional materials including event posters and banner layouts[cite: 1]. The main poster incorporates transparent logo variations, comic-style slime motifs, protagonist artwork, and physical card elements[cite: 1].
