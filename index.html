About

I, Nuno Baptista, am a Media Artist, with a master’s degree in the faculty of Fine Arts of Lisbon, University of Lisbon.

I am passionate about creating environment and characters that we, the players can interact with. I am particularly interested in the connection between the visual art and technology. There is a magic quality when working with a computer. I constantly seek to expand my knowledge, technique and culture.

Animation

I have animated in both 2D and 3D workflows, though my main focus is in 3D My focus have been mostly in animation for game development thought my technical expertise allows me to integrate more complex and powerful techniques, like Blender's crease pencil, mixing 2D and 3D workflows.

2D Art

I have studied in Lisbon Fine Art Faculty, learning traditional skills, like drawing and painting, proportion and anatomy. I've also drawn concept art for games and movies. I am proficient in both environments and characters, in many drawing programs.
